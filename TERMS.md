## Service scope and limitations

The Service scales selected, published process factors by quantities entered in each factor's stated reference unit. It reports individual process results and environmental indicators separately. It does **not** calculate a complete product life-cycle footprint, sum different process stages, produce a combined score, or provide a product comparison, certification, or verified environmental claim.

Source boundaries, geographies, reference units, data quality, and coverage vary. Results are estimates based on the identified published source and stated transformation; they may be incomplete, unsuitable for a particular decision, or different from another method. Check the selected factor, source notes, units, and limitations independently before relying on any result.

The Service is informational and is not legal, regulatory, engineering, investment, procurement, or professional life-cycle-assessment advice. It does not replace an independently scoped and reviewed assessment. Do not use a result alone to substantiate a regulated disclosure, environmental claim, product label, or compliance decision.
